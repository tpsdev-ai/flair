- **A successful bootstrap now returns a `skills` manifest and `skillDiagnostics`, over REST and native `/mcp`.** Each `skills` entry is `{ name, skillId, scope, priority, source }` for an assigned skill that won priority resolution; fetch its procedure with `skill_get` and the `skillId`. `skillDiagnostics` lists the assignments that were refused, superseded, unresolved or ambiguous, each with a reason. Both are present with `includeSoul: false` and at the `/mcp` default. They are charged to the `maxTokens` content budget and counted in `skillsTokens` and `skillDiagnosticsTokens`; an entry that does not fit is counted in `skillsTruncated` or `skillDiagnosticsTruncated`.

  The prose `## Active Skills` section now lists only the entries in `skills`, and only when both `includeSoul` and `includeContext` are true. In 0.58.0 it also listed refused and superseded assignments with a `SKILL_CONFLICT` or `refused` note, and assignments that named no skill row; those are now reported in `skillDiagnostics` instead.
