- **The dependency audit gate checks allowlist history from a pinned start at a fixed path.** `.github/audit-allowlist.json` must be JSON with an `entries` array at the pinned commit and every descendant in HEAD’s ancestry. An id’s first day is the earliest UTC day of a commit introducing it with no prior occurrence in ancestry within the pinned lineage; ids compare case-insensitively. Missing lineage or shallow history fails the gate.
