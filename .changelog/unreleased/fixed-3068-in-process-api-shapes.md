- **The in-process facade's read methods now resolve to the shapes they document.** `recall()` resolves to an array of hits, `memory.get()` resolves to the stored row or `null` when the record is absent or not readable by the calling agent (`flair.admin.memory.get()` resolves to the stored row or `null` for an absent id), and `memory.search()` resolves to an array of at most its `limit` rows; error responses reject instead of resolving to `{ error, status }`.
