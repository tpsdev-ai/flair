- **The A2A message/send and agent-key-register OrgEvent rows are validated before staging.** Their caller-derived fields are coerced to the schema's types where they are assigned; a failure answers the endpoint's server-error shape (500 `org_event_row_invalid` for A2A, 503 `registration_unavailable` for key registration) and commits nothing. The migration ledger and graph-heal OrgEvent writers validate their rows too.
