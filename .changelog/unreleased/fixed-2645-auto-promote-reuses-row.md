- **A retried auto-promote stamps the unstamped Memory row an earlier sweep recorded on its candidate instead of writing a second row.** The sweep reuses that row only when the recording was persisted and the row's `id` and `instanceToken` equal the recorded ones, its `agentId` is the sweeping agent, `promotionStatus` is unset, it is not archived, its `content` equals the claim, its `visibility` and `durability` equal what the sweep writes, and its `tags` include every promotion tag; otherwise the sweep writes and stamps a new row. The stamp re-checks these fields under the per-id Memory lock, and a row that no longer passes is refused with its candidate left pending. No other field is compared, and a same-id Memory write keeps the incarnation token (#2645).
