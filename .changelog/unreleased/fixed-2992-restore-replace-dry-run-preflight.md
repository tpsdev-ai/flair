- **`flair restore --replace --dry-run` runs preflight checks before reporting dry-run completion (#2992).**
  In `--replace --dry-run` mode, restore now runs the replace-only preflight and reports that preflight's refusals with the same diagnostics as a real replace, exiting non-zero without deleting or writing any records. When no refusals are encountered, it confirms dry-run completion and exits zero.
