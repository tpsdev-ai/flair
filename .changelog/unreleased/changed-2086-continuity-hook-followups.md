- **flair-client adds `loadPrivateKeyBounded` for asynchronous, size-capped key reads, and the continuity record's redaction covers more credential shapes (#2086).** The cap bounds the bytes read, and the read is asynchronous so a caller's own deadline can fire while it is pending. Redaction now also covers PGP `PRIVATE KEY BLOCK` blocks. New provider patterns begin at a word boundary: Stripe `[sr]k_(live|test)_` needs at least 16 letters or digits, `hf_` and `gsk_` need at least 20 letters or digits, and `pypi-` needs at least 16 letters, digits, underscores or hyphens. The credential-name pattern requires `:` or `=` and a nonempty value, so a bare `token` is untouched. Matching text can be redacted even when it is not a secret. A new integration test reads the SessionStart record through an ephemeral Harper.
